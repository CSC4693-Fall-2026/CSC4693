How to read it. Each gap is the median 2024 gross pay of Cal Poly SLO ladder faculty minus the median of bachelor's program-level earnings medians five years after completion, grouped by academic bucket. The overall average gives each bucket equal weight. Faculty and graduate earnings represent different populations and time periods.
