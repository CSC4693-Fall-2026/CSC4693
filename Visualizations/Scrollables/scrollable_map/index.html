CAL POLY BY MAJOR

The faculty–graduate comparison differs across majors.

This regular article figure compares the five largest faculty-over-graduate gaps with the five smallest or negative gaps among matched Cal Poly majors.

Proof-of-concept method. School earnings are institution-level aggregates of reported bachelor's-program medians. Because comparable faculty data is only available for Cal Poly, the statewide gap uses Cal Poly's median faculty base pay as the same benchmark for every institution; it is not each school's actual faculty pay. The Cal Poly major chart instead uses faculty total compensation by major.
